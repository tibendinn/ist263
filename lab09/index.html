Thalia Benton-Dinneen's Biography

My name is Thalia Benton-Dinneen. I am a Policy Studies and Information Management and Technology double major. I am most interested in local government work, with the use of data analytics to supplement policy design and analysis.

I previously interned for Rochester Housing Authority as a Resident Services Intern where I worked on a comprehensive eviction prevention plan for families with school aged children in Public and Section 8 Housing. This past summer, I worked for the City of Syracuse as a Neighborhood Planning Intern and am excited to continue this work into the school year.
