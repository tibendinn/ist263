Crispy Chicken Thighs & Mushroom Risotto

Prep time: 15 mins | Cook time: 30 mins

Risotto and crispy chicken is one of my favorite simple yet impressive dishes that can be made for any occasion!

Ingredients:

  • Skin on Bone in Chicken Thighs
  • Rosemary
  • 1 cup Arborio Rice
  • 4 cups Chicken Broth
  • 1 Yellow Onion
  • 1 lb Mushrooms
  • 3 Garlic Cloves
  • 2 TBSP Unsalted Butter
  • ½ Cup Dry White Wine
  • 1 Cup Shredded Parmesan
  • 2 TBSP Chopped Parsley
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt & Pepper

For the Chicken:

To start, remove the bone from the chicken thigh carefully. Salt your chicken on both sides and pepper only on the side without the skin to avoid burning the pepper.

Place your chicken skin side down on a lightly oiled stainless-steel pan. Make sure the pan is cold, do not pre-heat!

Let cook until the skin releases from the pan; the chicken will be seventy-five percent done at this point. Flip and add the spring of rosemary. Cook until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165 degrees F.

For the Risotto:

Heat a mid-to-large-size pan with a small amount of oil. Add the onions and mushrooms. Sauté until they release some of their liquid. Add the garlic, salt, & pepper and sauté for 1 additional minute. Set this mixture aside.

Melt a TBSP of butter and add risotto – toast lightly. Add the wine and let reduce – 2 minutes.

NOTE: For adding the broth, you have two options. You can boil the broth in a separate saucepan and add it to the risotto bit by bit. The logic is that this will yield a creamier and more balanced risotto, because the temperatures are all aligned. However, in the interest of saving dishes, I usually add the broth in at room temperature!

Regardless of your choice, add ½ cup of broth at a time. when you have ½ cup left, stir in parmesan & butter with the remaining broth and cook until the risotto reaches al dente. It is best to pull it a minute or two before you think it is ready as it will continue cooking.

Add the mushroom mixture back and combine. Top risotto with fresh parsley and add the chicken thigh – enjoy!
