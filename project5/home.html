Why Cook?

Cooking is more than a means to an end – it is a way to connect with people and produce something out of nothing. My favorite memories have been formed in the kitchen – from cooking with my mom to making truly something out of nothing with my freshman year roommate (microwaved cheesy nachos!).

I have gotten more adventurous with my cooking as I’ve connected with others who push me to try new techniques or simply cook with the burner on low … and be patient.

Why The Red Book?

A Great Red Book has sat it my family’s kitchen for as long as I can remember. It contains recipes from prior generations with recipe cards marked in my Yiayia’s signature uppercase handwriting. Although my Yiayia’s recipes were the first to enter the book, my mom has since added her own touch. Clippings from magazines and scrap pieces of paper began to populate the pages. A secret mac n’ cheese recipe entered the book as my mom grew closer to her grandmother in-law. The Red Book evolved and became a vault.

Red Book Cooking aims to reveal these recipes and share pieces of my families cooking with you!
